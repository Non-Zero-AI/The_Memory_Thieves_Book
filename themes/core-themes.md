# Core Themes

## Memory and Identity

If memories can be stolen, edited, copied, or hidden, what makes a person real?

## Forgetting as Mercy

The story asks whether forgetting is a flaw or a survival mechanism.

## Moral Ambiguity

The Archivists commit real crimes for a real reason.

The Curators offer real hope with catastrophic consequences.

## The Danger of Perfect Preservation

A civilization that never forgets may become powerful.

It may also become visible.

## Technology Becoming Mythology

The Echo Field allows the story to blur lines between science, magic, religion, and ancient myth.

## Death and Continuity

If a person is preserved in memory, are they alive?

If a collective remembers someone, does that create a soul or a copy?

## Hidden Layers

Every truth reveals another truth beneath it.

The plot should operate through escalating layers:

1. Personal mystery
2. Institutional conspiracy
3. Hidden realm
4. Factional war
5. Ancient civilization
6. Cosmic predator
7. Possible living universe
