# Master Timeline

Two clocks run in this story. Keep both.

- **Physical time** is linear and chronological.
- **Echo Field time is associative** — memories connect by emotion, symbol, and
  relationship, not by date. A scene inside the Echo Field can move sideways
  through meaning (`lore/echo-field.md`).

A third thing to track is **reader knowledge vs. character knowledge vs. true
chronology** — the reveal structure means the audience's understanding of the
past is reordered as the layers open (`plot/reveal-layers.md`).

Maintained by the `timeline-keeper` skill.

## Per-Book Chronological Anchors

- **Book 1 — The Memory Thieves:** Elias, Curator believer → discovers Archivists are hiding humanity from something.
- **Book 2 — The Containment Lie:** Archivist atrocities revealed; their doctrine is incomplete.
- **Book 3 — The Firstborn Memory:** the hidden memory awakens; Elias reaches the Deep Echo.
- **Book 4 — The Silence Was Not Death:** what really happened to the Firstborn.
- **Book 5 — The Field Remembers Back:** humanity overcomes the Predator by rousing the memory of everything it ever ate.
