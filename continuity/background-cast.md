# Background Cast

The recurring **minor and incidental figures** who keep the world alive — the bread
maker, the produce seller, the newspaper vendor. Not plot characters (those live in
`characters/`), but faces the reader sees more than once, so the world feels
continuous and lived-in.

Maintained alongside `scene-builder` (which registers and reuses these) and the craft
principle in `craft/living-world.md`. Reuse a returning face before inventing a new
one — a recurring figure is worth ten one-offs.

## How to use this file

- Add a figure the first time they're likely to recur (a one-scene extra needs no
  entry).
- Give each a **signature detail** so they're recognizable when they return.
- Note **where/when** they appear, and let them change over time — age, move on,
  vanish (and let someone notice they're gone).

## Registry

| Tag | Who | Signature detail | Appears near | Notes / arc |
|-----|-----|------------------|--------------|-------------|
| *(seed)* the corner baker | A baker on a market street Elias crosses | Slides loaves out on a long paddle; flour to the elbow; hums the same tune | Market street near the Quick / civilian districts | A returning face for the passage of time; could quietly age across books |
| *(seed)* the produce widow | A woman selling produce near Della's neighborhood | Counts coins twice; keeps the bruised fruit for the kids who linger | Della Okafor's district (`locations/della-apartment.md`) | Grounds the human cost of memory theft at street level |
| *(seed)* the Solarium groundskeeper | Tends the domed gardens that never go out of season | Talks to the plants; has worked there longer than anyone recalls | The Solarium (`locations/the-solarium.md`) | Ordinary labor beneath the Curators' grand promise; a witness who is never asked |

*(Seeds are starters — keep, rename, or replace them freely. Their job is to show the
format and give the first scenes a face or two to reach for.)*

## Ambient-life palette (quick-reach texture)

Not characters — a stock of world-texture to sprinkle, always mindful it can carry the
memory theme (someone remembering, forgetting, keeping):

- Street preachers of the Curators promising an end to grief
- Stall-keepers trading rumors about the "memory thieves"
- A grandmother losing a story she's telling a child
- Roadside memorials, grave-tending, walls of missing-person photos
- A person with a hole in their memory, passing in the background
