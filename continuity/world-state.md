# World State

Tracks what the *story* has actually visited and encountered, as opposed to what
the lore *describes*. Canon (`lore/`) is the catalog; this is the field log.

Maintained by `scene-builder` (adds locations/entities as they appear) and
`timeline-keeper`.

---

consistent each time it appears.

|----------|-----------------|-------------------|---------------|-------|

| Abyssal Boundary | — | Edge where memory begins to dissolve | — | Borders the Abyssal Quiet |

## Faction Status

| Faction | Public face | Actual aim | Story status |
|---------|-------------|-----------|--------------|
| Archivists | Memory thieves / hidden rulers (villains) | Containment — prevent memory density from waking the threat | Elias's enemy at Book 1 start |
| Curators | Visionaries, healers, liberators | Merge humanity with Echo entities to end death | Elias aligned with them at Book 1 start |

Both believe they are saving humanity. Both are wrong. Keep each argument
genuinely persuasive whenever a faction voice is on the page.
