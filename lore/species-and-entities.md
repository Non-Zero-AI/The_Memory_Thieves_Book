# Echo Field Species and Entities

## Drifters

The simplest Echo lifeforms.

They are clusters of forgotten memories.

They move like floating schools of fish and consume abandoned memory fragments.

Mostly harmless.

## Weavers

Ancient, spider-like entities made from connected memories.

They maintain pathways between memories.

Without them, memory structures decay faster.

Archivists secretly cultivate Weavers to stabilize safe corridors through the Echo Field.

## Keepers

Massive beings created from collective remembrance of major ideals.

Examples:

- Justice
- Honor
- Sacrifice
- Freedom
- Betrayal
- Mercy

They are not gods.

They are living archetypes.

## Mourning Giants

Entities created from collective grief.

Entire nations mourning together can accidentally create one.

They wander endlessly.

Every step causes nearby memories to resurface.

People who encounter them often relive their entire lives.

## Knots

Traumatic memories compressed into violent living storms.

They are dangerous and unpredictable.

Archivists often destroy or isolate them.

Curators sometimes attempt to integrate them.

## Saints

Memory entities created from billions of people remembering the same individual.

They are not the original person.

They are the collective memory of that person.

A Saint can be merciful, monstrous, distorted, or fragmented depending on how the person is remembered.

## Shadows

Entities born from collective fear.

Examples:

- War
- Disease
- Extinction
- Betrayal
- Abandonment
- Judgment
- The dark

Shadows are less like creatures and more like living pressure systems.

## Kings of Dust

Former memory entities that have eroded over time.

They are ancient, unstable, and insane.

They no longer remember what they are.

Only that they were once important.

## The Remembered

The Old Ones of the Echo Field.

Nobody knows whether they are native lifeforms, survivors, gods, or something else.

They were already present when the Firstborn arrived.

They are called the Remembered because nobody created them, yet somehow they exist.

## The Ancient Predator

The deepest suspected threat.

It may feed on civilizations when they reach sufficient memory density.

It may have consumed thousands of intelligent species.

It may not be a monster in the ordinary sense.

It may be a process, a lifeform, a cosmic immune response, or something older than memory itself.
