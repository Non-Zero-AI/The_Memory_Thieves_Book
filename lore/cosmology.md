# Cosmology

There are three known layers of existence.

## The Physical

The world of matter.

Humans believe this is reality.

It is only the surface.

The Physical is governed by:

- Matter
- Energy
- Space
- Time
- Biology
- Causality

## The Echo Field

The world of memory.

It is formed from meaning, identity, memory, emotion, and association.

Every intelligent species discovered so far eventually becomes connected to it.

Most civilizations mistake it for:

- An afterlife
- A collective unconscious
- A spiritual plane
- A god realm
- A dream world

It is none of those things exactly.

## The Abyssal Quiet

A region beneath the Echo Field.

Almost nothing is known about it.

Memory dissolves there.

Identity dissolves there.

Even Echo entities avoid it.

The oldest surviving records describe it with one recurring phrase:

> The place where remembering ends.

## The Firstborn

The Firstborn are the oldest known civilization whose memory traces still survive.

Nobody knows their real name.

They were not humanoid.

They resembled living crystal forests that became self-aware.

Their communities grew together physically and mentally.

Individuality existed, but it was fluid.

They shared memory naturally.

## The Firstborn Achievement

The Firstborn fully mapped the Echo Field before humanity existed.

They discovered:

- Memory can be preserved
- Identity can be copied
- Consciousness can be transferred
- Death can be delayed
- Civilization can be encoded into shared memory structures

They believed they had defeated mortality.

They were wrong.

## The Silence

One day, Firstborn records stop.

Not gradually.

Instantly.

No wars.

No collapse.

No decline.

Just nothing.

An entire galactic civilization vanished.

## What Remains

Their memory structures still exist in the Deep Echo.

These ruins are like abandoned cities built from thought.

Some are larger than planets.
