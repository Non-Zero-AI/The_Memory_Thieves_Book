# Resonance Technology

How humans reach the Echo Field, find what is written there, and change it.

This is the engine under the whole story. It is built to obey one hard rule: the
Echo Field is made of **meaning, not energy** (`lore/cosmology.md`). So the tech does
not *force* a crossing. It *tunes* one.

---

## First Principle: Alignment, Not Transport

Access works by **Resonance** — recreating a memory's unique pattern precisely enough
that a person's *awareness* shifts into the Echo Field while their body stays behind
(`lore/memory-rules.md`, Rules 1 and 3). The Archivists developed the method; the
Curators built their own application of it.

Nothing travels. The body does not cross. Consciousness **aligns** to a pattern, and
the Field unfolds around the aligned mind (`lore/echo-field.md`). A traveler is less
like an astronaut and more like a radio finally tuned to a station that was always
broadcasting.

## It Is Tuned, Not Powered

This is the rule that makes the technology feel like *this* world and no other.

You cannot buy depth with electricity. A crossing's reach — Surface Echo, Deep Echo,
or the ruins below — is bought with **precision and significance**, never with raw
power. The rig's electricity runs its sensors and its pattern-computation; that is
all. What actually determines how far and how safely you go is the quality of three
non-electrical things:

1. **The resonance signature** — the *key*. A recorded, sampled, or reconstructed
   pattern of the specific memory, mind, or place you are tuning to. Precision here is
   everything; a rough key gets you a blurred, shallow, dangerous crossing.
2. **The anchor** — a significance-dense object or memory that gives the alignment
   something heavy to hold onto. The Field respects significance, not mass, so a
   murder weapon, a wedding ring, or a childhood home makes a stronger anchor than
   anything you could manufacture.
3. **The tether** — the lifeline of resonance kept running back to the body and the
   point of entry, so the traveler can find the way home. Lose the tether and you are
   not dead; you are *lost*, which in the Echo Field is worse.

A mind, a key, an anchor, a tether. Power the machine all you like — without those,
it does nothing.

## What It Looks Like

The same principle, dressed by two philosophies:

- **Curator cradles** (as at the Solarium) — immersive, comfortable, almost devotional.
  Reclined couches wrapped in sensory reconstruction and gentle neural coupling, built
  to sink a mind *deep* and make merging feel like homecoming. Beautiful, and built to
  make you stop asking questions.
- **Archivist rigs** (as at the Quiet House) — spare, clinical, surgical. Restraints,
  monitors, a single clean interface. Built for control, for going in, doing one exact
  thing, and getting out with the smallest possible footprint.
- **Field resonators** — portable units carried by operatives like Elias. They reach
  only the Surface Echo, hold only briefly, and run hot with risk. A strong personal
  anchor object substitutes for a fixed installation's calibration.
- **Unaided alignment** — the rarest case. A few gifted minds can align with little or
  no equipment at all. It is the deepest, oldest, and least controllable way in. The
  Firstborn did it *biologically*, as a species. That a human can approach it is a
  danger sign, not a gift.

**Portability spectrum:** fixed cradle/rig (reaches the Deep Echo, safest) → field
resonator (Surface Echo only, short, risky) → unaided (deepest, uncontrolled). Which
tier a scene uses says something about who is in it and what they are willing to
spend.

## Echo Location — Finding Something in the Field

Every memory leaves a unique resonance (Rule 1), so *finding* one is a matter of
acquiring its signature and tuning to it. A hunter needs a sample of the target's
pattern — pulled from an anchor object, a biological trace, a name freighted with
enough significance, or a fragment of the memory itself. This is how Elias hunts:
Resonance mapping to locate a mind or a memory in the vast associative dark of the
Field.

Because the Field is associative, you can also find things *sideways* — following the
emotional and symbolic links from a memory you have to the one you want. It is slower,
stranger, and it is how you find things no one gave you a key to.

## Echo Manipulation — Changing What Is Written

Once aligned to a memory's structure, a skilled operator can:

- **Extract** — copy a memory out, leaving the original (theft that the victim may not
  even notice at first).
- **Erase** — destroy the Echo record itself. The most feared act, because it is the
  most permanent.
- **Edit** — rewrite a memory or splice in false continuity, so a life still *feels*
  seamless around a lie.
- **Implant** — place a memory that was never lived (what Kellan did to Elias, at a
  level of craft almost no one has).
- **Contain** — wall off a memory-structure that has grown dangerous, isolating it in
  an engineered dead zone (the Archivists' containment work; see the Quiet House).

These map directly onto the Archivists' listed methods (`factions/archivists.md`).
The tech is what makes each of them a repeatable procedure instead of an accident.

## How the Echo Reaches Back Into the Body

This is why memory theft is *real* and not metaphor.

The brain does not fully store a memory. It **indexes** to it — it holds the physical
trace that points at the memory's imprint in the Echo Field, and normal recall reads
from both at once (memories exist in both layers — `lore/cosmology.md`). The two are
coupled.

So changing the Echo record propagates back into the person:

- **Damage the record** and physical recall retrieves static, or nothing → the memory
  is gone.
- **Edit the record** and the brain faithfully reads the forgery → a false past that
  feels true.

The mind does not notice the seam, because the mind is only the *reader*. Whoever
controls the record controls what is read.

## Memory, Forgetting, and Illness

The most important distinction in the whole system — and the one that keeps this
world from becoming absurd.

Natural forgetting and deliberate manipulation are two different failures of the
**same coupling**:

- **Natural degradation** — age, dementia, amnesia, trauma. The brain's *index*
  frays; its pointer to the Echo record decays. Crucially, **the memory itself may
  still be intact in the Field** — the person simply can no longer reach it. This is
  the mechanistic heart of the Curator promise: *"Your mother is not gone. She is
  still written. We can restore the way back to her."* It is also Mara Sennet's wound
  and the reason her belief is unshakeable.
- **Deliberate manipulation** — erasure, editing, theft. This does not fray the index;
  it attacks the **record**. Erasure is categorically worse than any disease, because
  dementia only hides what erasure annihilates.

This answers the obvious question cleanly: people suffered amnesia, dementia, and
fractured identity long before anyone built a resonance rig, because **the coupling is
ancient and the tech is new**. Humans have always been faintly connected to the Field
(Rule 2). The machines did not create the wound; they are the first tools precise
enough to reach into it — to heal it, or to cut.

*(Fractured or parallel identity is treated the same way: a natural fragmentation of a
mind's Echo-structure, not something the tech invents. Handle with care and never as a
gimmick.)*

## The Cost

Resonance is not free to the traveler. Sustained or deep crossings slowly fray the
traveler's *own* index — deep travelers risk forgetting their own lives. This is not a
side effect; it is the price, and it should always be on the table when a character
goes deep.

It is why **Silas Thorne** is hollowed: a man who has spent decades in the Field, and
erased so much — including, deliberately, parts of himself — that he is more absence
than person. It is why the safest operators go shallow, and why going deep is a
statement about how much a character is willing to lose.

## The Mythic Shadow

Humanity did not discover the Echo Field with this technology. It has been brushing
against it forever (Rule 2), and it gave those accidental contacts other names.

Astral projection, remote viewing, prophecy, séance, ancestral visions, déjà vu — in
this world these are not separate magic. They are folklore's crude, involuntary,
low-fidelity Echo contact: what happens when an untrained mind grazes the Field by
accident. Resonance technology is simply the first *reliable* version of a thing
mystics have fumbled at for millennia.

This is deliberate, and it matters for tone: the tech is **not** literal astral
projection bolted onto a sci-fi frame. It is the science that the old myths were
always dimly describing — the story's theme of *technology becoming mythology*
(`themes/core-themes.md`) read backwards.
