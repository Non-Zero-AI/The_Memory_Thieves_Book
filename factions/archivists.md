# The Archivists

## Public Reputation

The Archivists are believed to be memory thieves and hidden rulers.

They are accused of:

- Stealing memories
- Erasing identities
- Manipulating governments
- Rewriting criminal histories
- Destroying evidence
- Assassinating scientists
- Suppressing technology

The public views them as villains.

## Actual Purpose

The Archivists are a containment organization.

They believe memory density can awaken or attract the ancient threat beneath the Echo Field.

Their mission is to prevent humanity from reaching the threshold that destroyed previous civilizations.

## Methods

The Archivists use:

- Memory extraction
- Memory erasure
- Resonance mapping
- Echo Field containment zones
- Disinformation
- Assassination
- Destruction of dangerous research
- Controlled forgetting

## Moral Position

The Archivists believe freedom without containment leads to extinction.

They accept being hated if it means humanity survives.

## Their Weakness

They have become so committed to containment that they may no longer know the difference between protection and control.

They may be preserving humanity by slowly hollowing it out.

## Internal Conflict

Some Archivists believe the organization has gone too far.

Others believe it has not gone far enough.

Elias's father belonged to a faction that discovered both the Archivists and Curators were being manipulated.
