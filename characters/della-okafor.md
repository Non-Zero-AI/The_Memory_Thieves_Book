# Della Okafor

## Role

The inciting case. A civilian whose memory was stolen — and whose theft doesn't fit
the story either faction tells. Della is the thread Elias pulls. Assigned to her case
as routine Curator work, he finds an anomaly in what was taken from her, and the
anomaly is what starts him doubting everything.

She is also the human cost of memory theft given a face: not an abstraction, a person
grieving a hole she can feel but not name.

## Working Name

Della Okafor.

## Background

An oral-history archivist — she spent her life recording other people's memories,
sitting with the elderly and the dying so their stories wouldn't vanish. There is a
bitter symmetry in the fact that someone came and took one of hers.

She noticed the theft the way you notice a missing tooth with your tongue: a place
her mind keeps returning to and finding empty. She knows something was there. She
cannot prove it, and the not-knowing is eating her.

## Faction and Belief

Unaligned. Della is a civilian caught between the factions, which is exactly what
makes her useful to the story — she has no doctrine to defend, only a loss to
understand. If she leans anywhere, it is toward the Curator promise, because she of
all people knows what it is to lose a memory and want it back.

## Relationship to Memory and the Echo Field

No special access — an ordinary mind with an extraordinary hole in it. But the memory
that was taken from her left a residue in the Echo Field, and that residue is
anomalous: it carries a resonance that shouldn't be in a civilian's life at all — a
faint echo of the same impossible thing that haunts Elias's dreams.

## The Secret

The stolen memory itself, which neither Della nor Elias can yet read. What Elias
recovers of it points two dangerous directions at once:

- Toward an Archivist containment operation — evidence that the "thieves" were doing
  something stranger than theft (seed of layer 3).
- Toward the same deep resonance Elias carries — a thread connecting an ordinary
  woman to the Firstborn mystery (seed of layers 7–8).

Why *her* memory carries that resonance is one of the questions the theft opens.

## Voice

Searching, unsettled, precise about everything except the thing she has lost. A
professional rememberer undone by a single act of forgetting. Grief without a body to
bury.

## Character Arc

### Beginning
A case file. A routine assignment that should have taken Elias an afternoon.

### Middle
A person — the assignment becomes a face, and the face makes the cost of memory theft
impossible for Elias to keep treating as policy.

### Later
Her stolen memory proves pivotal: a small, ordinary theft that turns out to be a
seam in the larger secret. What was taken from Della matters more than anyone erased
it believed.
