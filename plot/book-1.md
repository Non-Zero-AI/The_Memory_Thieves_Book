# Book 1 — The Memory Thieves

**Logline:** A Curator memory-hunter chasing the Archivists discovers the "villains"
are jailers, holding humanity back from something waiting in the dark.

**Lands:** L1, L2 established; **L3** (Archivists are containment) at the finale.
**Master misdirection set:** *the villains are the jailers.*

---

## Main Plot

Elias Voss is a rising Curator operative who hunts Archivists and recovers or erases
the memories they steal. A routine assignment — the theft suffered by an oral
historian, Della Okafor — carries an anomaly: her stolen memory left a resonance that
has no business in a civilian's life. Pulling that thread leads Elias to the Archivist
Silas Thorne, and into a cat-and-mouse that steadily dismantles the story Elias has
been told about who the monsters are. By the finale, cornered, Thorne reveals the
truth the whole faction is built to hide: the Archivists are not thieves for power.
They are a containment order, keeping humanity below a threshold that once destroyed
something vast. Elias's crimes were real; his enemies' motive was protection.

## Act Structure & Key Scenes

**Act I — The Believer**
- Open on a clean memory-theft op: establish the tech, the world, the cost — *everyone
  hollows a little going deep; Elias doesn't.* Seed the anomaly quietly. (`locations/the-quiet-house.md` counterpart: a Curator cradle at the Solarium.)
- Home beat with Mara: the Curator argument at its most loving; the too-tidy house; no
  photograph of a father. (`locations/voss-home.md`)
- Renn assigns the Della case. **Inciting thread:** the anomalous resonance in Della's
  wound. (`locations/della-apartment.md`)

**Act II — The Hunt**
- Chasing Thorne; first sight of a containment site and the human cost of erasure.
- **Midpoint twist:** the recovered fragment of Della's memory implicates the *Curator*
  story, not just an Archivist crime. First crack in Elias's certainty. *(Misdirection:
  reader suspects a rogue Archivist; the rot is systemic.)*
- A deep dive to chase Thorne through the Field — Elias reaches deeper than should be
  possible and does not hollow. Something in the Deep *notices.*
- Low point: Elias corners Thorne (or is caught by him); the hunt collapses into a
  standoff.

**Act III — The Jailer's Truth**
- **Climax / L3:** Thorne reveals containment — the Archivists hold humanity back from
  what memory density attracts. Not a lie; a confession. It recolors every "crime"
  Elias has witnessed.
- **Ends on:** Elias belongs to no one now. And his impossible dive did not go
  unwitnessed — the dark is aware of him. → Book 2.

## Twists & Turns

- The hunted man is the truth-teller.
- The victim's stolen memory implicates Elias's own side.
- Elias himself is the anomaly he's been trained to explain away.
