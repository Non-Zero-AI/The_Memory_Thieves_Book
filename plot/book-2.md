# Book 2 — The Containment Lie

**Logline:** Freed of both sides, Elias learns the Archivists' atrocities are real and
their doctrine is a lie of omission — and that someone has been playing both factions
from the beginning.

**Lands:** L4, L5 solidify; **L6** lands — *as a human conspiracy* (a true but
incomplete face). **Master misdirection set:** *the conspiracy is human.*

---

## Main Plot

Loyal to no one, Elias goes underground in an uneasy alliance with Thorne, and learns
the full weight of what containment costs: not surgical mercy but mass erasure, whole
communities quietly hollowed, a strategy that only ever *delays* and keeps humanity
ignorant and defenceless in the bargain. In parallel he pulls on the thread of his own
father. Kellan Voss was an Archivist — one of the best — who discovered that the war
between the factions is rigged: both were shaped toward the same end. He was erased for
it, and before he vanished he hid something in his son. As the Curators, driven by
Renn, push toward a large-scale merger event, Elias uncovers the manipulation. In this
book it wears a human face — a hidden origin common to both factions. That face is
true. It is not the bottom.

## Act Structure & Key Scenes

**Act I — Two Bad Answers**
- The Elias/Thorne alliance: distrust, and two arguments that are each too persuasive.
  Contrast containment and transcendence with no clean winner.
- Discover containment's real scale — an erased town, a hollowed witness. The cost of
  the "necessary" evil made concrete and unbearable.

**Act II — The Father-Shaped Hole**
- Investigate Kellan: return to the Voss house and its sealed Echo wing; Mara's edited
  grief surfaces (SP-8); recover the first true fragments of him.
- The Curator thread rises: Renn advancing the merger project; Della's stolen memory
  turns out to be a key, not a footnote (SP-6).
- **Midpoint twist:** the founding records of *both* factions share an origin. The war
  was authored. **L6 lands — as human conspiracy.** *(Misdirection: the audience gets a
  satisfying villain — a cabal — and stops digging. Book 4 reveals the cabal was itself
  steered.)*
- Low point: an atrocity Elias is complicit in or cannot stop; someone within reach is
  hollowed or lost. His hands are no longer clean either.

**Act III — The Vault**
- Kellan's fate resolves to *erased, not dead* — possibly still out there. His crime
  was knowing. His last act was hiding the memory in Elias.
- **Climax:** Elias realizes he is carrying something — the edge of L8, not yet its
  content. To learn what, he must go into the Deep.
- **Ends on:** the hidden memory stirs; the only way forward is down. → Book 3.

## Twists & Turns

- Containment is real *and* insufficient — being right isn't the same as being enough.
- The war is rigged; the factions are two hands of one authorship.
- Kellan is erased, not dead — and Elias is the vault he built.

## Threads Advanced

Solidifies L4 (memories become entities, now field-tested) and L5 (the Curators'
sympathetic, dangerous argument); lands **L6** as human conspiracy; thickens L7 dread.
Pays SP-2 and SP-8 partially; advances SP-6.

## Ends On

Both answers humanity has are wrong, the war was written by someone, Elias's father
built him into a hiding place — and the thing hidden is about to wake.
