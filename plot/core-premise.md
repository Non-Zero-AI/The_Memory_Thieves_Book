# Core Premise

## The Memory Thieves

Human memories are not stored solely in the brain.

They exist in a second, hidden layer of reality called **the Echo Field**.

Every experience, emotion, trauma, secret, invention, and decision leaves an imprint there. Most people never learn this. A few do. An even smaller number learn how to manipulate it.

## Public Understanding

The world eventually learns of a secret organization known as **the Archivists**.

The Archivists are believed to:

- Steal memories
- Erase identities
- Extract secrets
- Manipulate governments
- Rewrite criminal histories
- Destroy evidence
- Silence dangerous witnesses

They are considered the monsters behind history's missing truths.

## The Hidden Truth

The Archivists are not stealing memories for power.

They are stealing them for containment.

They have discovered that memories do not simply record events.

Memories can create entities.

## The Core Moral Engine

The strongest version of this story is not good guys versus bad guys.

It is:

> Everyone believes they are saving humanity.
>
> Everyone is right.
>
> Everyone is wrong.

The story follows morally compromised people justifying horrific acts because they believe those acts prevent an even greater catastrophe.

## The Deeper Threat

Humanity did not create the Echo Field.

Humanity merely connected to it.

Something else was already there.

Waiting.
