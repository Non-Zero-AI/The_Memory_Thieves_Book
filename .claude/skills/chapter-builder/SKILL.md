# Chapter Builder

A chapter is a unit of momentum: it should open with a hook, turn on its middle,
and end on a change that pulls the reader forward. Build it to serve the series
arc, not just the page.

## Step 1 — Orient

- `plot/series-arc.md` and `plot/reveal-layers.md` — what this book and this
  chapter must accomplish
- `continuity/threads.md` — reveal-layer status and open setups/payoffs
- `continuity/timeline.md` — where the chapter falls
- `continuity/character-state/` — arc stage of the characters involved
- `themes/core-themes.md` — the ideas the chapter should press on

## Step 2 — Define the chapter

1. **Premise & function** — one or two sentences: what happens, and why it matters
   to the book's arc. Which book is this, and where in it?
2. **POV & span** — whose chapter is it? What span of time does it cover
   (Physical), and does it enter the Echo Field?
3. **Reveal targets** — which reveal layer(s) or setups does this chapter advance,
   plant, or pay off? Name them from `threads.md`. Not every chapter reveals, but
   every chapter should *move* something.
4. **Entry / exit state** — where the POV and the central relationships stand at
   the chapter's open vs. close. The delta is the chapter's job.

## Step 3 — Beat sheet & scene list

Break the chapter into scenes. For each scene give:

| # | Scene purpose | POV | Cast | Location (Physical / Echo) | Advances |
|---|---------------|-----|------|----------------------------|----------|

Shape the **tension curve**: rising stakes, a midpoint turn, a complication, and a
closing button (a reversal, a question, or a cost). Vary rhythm — a quiet,
memory-soaked Echo scene can sit between two sharp ones.

Keep the moral engine alive: if the chapter features a faction, let its argument be
genuinely persuasive. Keep each reveal in service of making the *previous* truth
feel smaller, not false.

## Step 4 — Write it out

Create `manuscript/chapters/chNN-<slug>/outline.md` containing the premise, POV,
reveal targets, entry/exit state, and the beat sheet.

## Step 5 — Wire into continuity

- `timeline.md` — add the chapter's chronological anchor.
- `threads.md` — register planned plants/payoffs and advance reveal status.

## Step 6 — Hand off to scene-building

Offer to build each beat into a scene with the `scene-builder` skill, in order.
`scene-builder` will keep the character ledgers and world-state current as it goes.
