# Della's Apartment

*The home of a woman who spent her life keeping other people's memories — with one of
her own cut cleanly out of the middle.*

## Physical Layer

A modest apartment crowded with the tools of an oral historian's vocation: shelves of
labeled recordings, boxes of tape and transcripts, a good microphone on a scarred
desk, walls of photographs of strangers — the elderly, the dying, the ordinary —
whose stories Della sat with so they would not vanish. It is warm, cluttered, deeply
human, the home of someone whose life's work was *against forgetting*.

One spot on one shelf is tidy in a way the rest of the room is not. A gap that has
been closed up. Della's eyes go to it and slide off, go to it and slide off, all day.

**Sensory palette:** paper and old tape, coffee gone cold, the click and hiss of
playback, lamplight, the particular stillness of a room organized around listening.

## Echo Field Layer

In the Echo Field the apartment is luminous with *borrowed* memory — every life Della
ever recorded lends the place a quiet, secondhand radiance, a warm chorus of other
people's rememberings held with care. It is a generous structure; she made it by
paying attention.

At its center is a wound. Not a dim spot, not decay — a *cauterized* absence, edges
sealed smooth and deliberate, where a single memory of her own was surgically taken.
The theft has a shape, and around that shape, wrong and faint, is a resonance that has
no business in a civilian's life: a note that belongs to crystal forests and silent
ruins, to something older than any story she ever archived. Whoever took the memory
may not have known what it was tangled up with.

## Significance (what weights the Echo layer)

Preservation, attention, the love of a person who kept others from being lost — and,
cut into it, one precise, surgical theft that matters far more than its size.

## Story Hooks

- **Della Okafor** — the wound is hers; the room is her portrait (SP-6).
- **Elias Voss** — the anomalous resonance rhymes with his own visions; the case is
  where his doubt begins (SP-1, SP-6).
- **The larger plot** — the theft points at an Archivist containment operation
  (layer 3) *and* at the Firstborn resonance (layers 7–8).
