# The Voss House

*Elias's childhood home — a happy house with one room the world refuses to remember.*

## Physical Layer

A modest, sunlit family house on an ordinary street. A kitchen that always smells
faintly of toast and citrus; a reading nook worn into the shape of a growing boy; a
small back garden where a swing has rusted at exactly the pace a childhood should.

It is warm, unremarkable, and just slightly too tidy — the tidiness of a home that
has been *curated*, though no one who lives there would say so. There are photographs
on the walls: Mara, Elias, birthdays, holidays. There is no photograph of a father,
and no one in the house has ever noticed the absence.

**Sensory palette:** warm light, soft wood, the low hum of a lived-in kitchen, dust
in sunbeams, the specific quiet of a house with one person too few in it.

## Echo Field Layer

In the Echo Field the house is enormous — a sprawling cathedral of ordinary joy.
Birthdays stack into vaulted ceilings; Christmas mornings hang like chandeliers;
a decade of Mara's love runs through the walls as warm structural light. By the logic
of significance, this small house is a landmark. A happy childhood is a *heavy* thing.

But one axis of the cathedral is wrong. There is a wing where the corridors do not
connect — where a whole load-bearing presence has been taken out and the ceiling
holds up anyway, which should not be possible. Rooms that ought to blaze with a
parent stand dim and papered-over, their edges too smooth, like a wall rebuilt over a
door. A trained traveler feels the seam before they see it: the house remembers being
happy and does not remember *why the happiness had the shape it did.*

This is not decay. Decay is honest. This is editing.

## Significance (what weights the Echo layer)

Love, safety, the density of a childhood — and, cut into the middle of it, a
deliberate erasure. The house is heavy with everything Elias was allowed to keep and
haunted by the exact volume of what was removed.

## Story Hooks

- **Kellan Voss** — the father-shaped void *is* him; the sealed wing is where his
  memory was scoured (SP-2).
- **Mara Sennet** — the missing photographs, the too-smooth edges: her editing,
  framed as mercy (SP-8).
- **Elias Voss** — his planted visions (crystal forests, silent ruins) bleed through
  the seams of this house more than anywhere else (SP-1).

## Continuity Notes

- Zone: Surface Echo (a personal memory structure).
- The sealed wing must stay *felt but unreadable* until its gated reveals — never
  narrate what was removed, only the shape of the removal.
